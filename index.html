Alecrim Rosemary Oil

100% Pure | For Thicker & Longer Hair

Ingredients

Rosemary oil, Castor oil, Coconut oil, Lavender oil, Peppermint oil, Vitamin E

Benefits

- Stimulates hair growth
- Strengthens hair roots
- Reduces dandruff
- Nourishes dry scalp
- Adds shine and softness

Directions

Apply a small amount to scalp and hair. Gently massage. Leave for 30 minutes or overnight. Rinse with mild shampoo. Use 2-3 times a week.

Warnings

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Keep out of reach of children. Do a patch test before use.
